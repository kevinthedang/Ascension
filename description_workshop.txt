[h3]A Roguelike Mod for tModLoader[/h3]

[b]Ascension[/b] completely reshapes the traditional Terraria experience into an action-packed, fast-paced roguelike adventure. Prepare your build, select your path, and face escalating combat encounters where every choice matters. 

[hr][/hr]

[h2]The Core Gameplay Loop[/h2]
[olist]
[*] [b]The Hub:[/b] Spawn in a safe lobby to prepare your character.
[*] [b]Choose Your Path:[/b] Select your next destination and prepare for combat.
[*] [b]Combat Encounters:[/b] Enter instanced, procedurally connected rooms filled with threats.
[*] [b]Conquer and Defeat:[/b] Fight your way through regular enemies, challenging Elites, and Biome Bosses.
[*] [b]Adapt and Evolve:[/b] Earn powerful run-specific relics, status effects, and stat upgrades.
[*] [b]Synergize:[/b] Chain unique offensive and defensive mechanics to create broken, unstoppable builds.
[*] [b]Deconstruct the Run:[/b] Defeat the final boss... or die trying, reset, and start fresh with a completely different loadout!
[/olist]
